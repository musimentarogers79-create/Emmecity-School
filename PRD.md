# Product Requirements

## Purpose
Provide EMMECTY NURSERY & PRIMARY SCHOOL with one secure, responsive system for daily administration, learning, finance, communication, and pupil welfare.

## Users
Administrators, super administrators, teachers, accountants, guardians, pupils, and support staff. Each role receives only its permitted navigation, data, and actions.

## Main modules
Role dashboards; pupil, guardian, user, staff, class, and subject records; attendance; academics and report cards; timetables; fees, receipts, expenses, and salaries; announcements; assignments; discipline; medical; library; transport; inventory; events; documents; reports; notifications; settings; audit-ready records.

## Core requirements
- Staff and teachers sign in with usernames; pupils and guardians use permanent registration identifiers.
- Administrators manage users. Teachers access only assigned classes and pupils.
- Pupil enrollment supports day or boarding status, term fees, and an optional opening payment.
- Positive opening payments create unique printable receipts.
- Registration and receipt numbers are generated safely per school and year.
- All runtime records persist in PostgreSQL; production starts clean except for the bootstrap administrator.
- Currency defaults to UGX and is configurable by an administrator.
- Visible terminology uses “Pupil/Pupils,” not “Student/Students.”

## Quality requirements
Responsive from mobile through desktop, accessible forms and navigation, clear loading/error states, role authorization on the server, secure cookies, hashed passwords and sessions, login throttling, database transactions for money and enrollment, and no personal data in analytics events.

## Business rules
Payments cannot exceed a pupil’s outstanding balance. Sensitive pupil records are limited to authorized staff or linked guardians/pupils. Data deletion, schema changes, and production bootstrap changes require deliberate review and backups.# Product Requirements

- Staff and teachers sign in with usernames; pupils and guardians use permanent registration identifiers.
- Administrators manage users. Teachers access only assigned classes and pupils.
- Pupil enrollment supports day or boarding status, term fees, and an optional opening payment.
- Positive opening payments create unique printable receipts.
- Registration and receipt numbers are generated safely per school and year.
- All runtime records persist in PostgreSQL; production starts clean except for the bootstrap administrator.
- Currency defaults to UGX and is configurable by an administrator.
- Visible terminology uses “Pupil/Pupils,” not “Student/Students.”

## Quality requirements
Responsive from mobile through desktop, accessible forms and navigation, clear loading/error states, role authorization on the server, secure cookies, hashed passwords and sessions, login throttling, database transactions for money and enrollment, and no personal data in analytics events.

## Business rules
Payments cannot exceed a pupil’s outstanding balance. Sensitive pupil records are limited to authorized staff or linked guardians/pupils. Data deletion, schema changes, and production bootstrap changes require deliberate review and backups.# Product Requirements
