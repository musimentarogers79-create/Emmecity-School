---
name: Legacy term reconciliation
description: Why older school fees and receipts cannot be silently moved into the new academic-term ledger.
---

Do not infer the academic year or term for pre-transition payments and unpaid pupil balances. Keep them visible as legacy amounts until an authorized staff member confirms their original period or an explicit opening-balance treatment.

**Why:** Older records do not reliably identify a year or term. Guessing from payment date or current class could mix terms, double-count a debt, or rewrite a pupil's historical account.

**How to apply:** When connecting legacy finance views to academic statements or dashboards, show the unattributed portion separately, record who made each attribution and its reason, and reconcile totals before treating the new ledger as the sole source of outstanding fees.
