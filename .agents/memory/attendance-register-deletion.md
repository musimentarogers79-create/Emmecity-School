---
name: Attendance register deletion
description: How deleted attendance registers must remain reviewable.
---

Deleting an attendance register is a soft archive: active attendance excludes its rows, while the deleted-register history retains the date, class, record count, deletion time, and required reason.

**Why:** School records need a visible explanation for removed registers instead of silently losing the audit context.

**How to apply:** Require a reason before the administrator or assigned teacher can archive a register, and keep the reason visible in the attendance history.
