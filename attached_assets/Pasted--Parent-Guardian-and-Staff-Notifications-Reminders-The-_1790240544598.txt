### Parent, Guardian and Staff Notifications & Reminders

The school management system must have a complete **automated communication and notification system** that keeps parents, guardians, staff, and other authorized users informed about all important school activities and financial information.

The system should automatically send notifications through **SMS, email, and in-system notifications** where available.

#### 1. School Events and Announcements

* When the school creates an event, the system should automatically notify the relevant parents/guardians and staff.
* Remind them before the event according to the school's configured schedule, for example:

  * 7 days before
  * 3 days before
  * 1 day before
  * On the event day
* Examples include:

  * Parents' meetings
  * Staff meetings
  * Sports days
  * Academic days
  * School trips
  * Examinations
  * Graduation
  * School closures
  * Opening and closing dates
  * Religious or cultural events
  * Important school announcements

#### 2. School Fees Balance Reminders

The system should automatically notify parents/guardians about their child's outstanding school fees balance.

Notifications should include:

* Student's name
* Student registration number
* Class
* Total fees required
* Amount paid
* Outstanding balance
* Payment deadline
* Available payment methods

The system should be able to send configurable reminders, such as:

* Before the payment deadline
* On the payment deadline
* After the deadline
* Periodic balance reminders until the balance is cleared

#### 3. Payment Confirmation

Whenever a payment is recorded, the system must automatically notify the parent/guardian.

The confirmation should include:

* Student's name
* Registration number
* Amount paid
* Payment date and time
* Payment method
* Receipt/payment reference number
* Previous balance
* New remaining balance
* Staff/user who recorded the payment, where appropriate

The parent/guardian should receive the confirmation immediately after the payment is successfully recorded.

#### 4. Other Important Notifications

The system should also support automated notifications for:

* Student registration confirmation
* Student admission
* New student account creation
* Password reset/OTP
* Attendance alerts
* Student absence notifications
* Late-coming notifications
* Examination results
* Report cards/results availability
* Assignment or academic updates
* Disciplinary notices
* Medical/health-related school alerts where appropriate
* School timetable changes
* Class changes
* Teacher changes
* Fee structure changes
* Important school announcements
* School closure/opening reminders
* Emergency announcements
* Lost-and-found notices
* Transport/bus updates where applicable

#### 5. Staff Notifications

Staff should receive relevant notifications about:

* Staff meetings
* School events
* Assigned duties
* Timetable changes
* Leave/approval decisions
* Important administrative announcements
* Payroll/salary notifications where applicable
* Department or class responsibilities
* Upcoming deadlines
* Tasks assigned to them

#### 6. Notification Management

Administrators should have a dedicated **Notifications & Communication** section where authorized users can:

* Create announcements
* Schedule notifications
* Select recipients
* Send notifications immediately
* Send to all parents/guardians
* Send to selected classes
* Send to selected students
* Send to selected staff
* Send to individual users
* Schedule recurring reminders
* Create notification templates
* Enable/disable specific automatic notifications
* Choose SMS, email, in-system notification, or multiple channels
* View sent notifications
* View delivery status
* View failed messages
* Resend failed notifications
* Maintain a communication history

#### 7. Automatic Financial Communication

The system must connect the **fees/payment module directly to the notification system**.

Whenever a financial event occurs, the appropriate parent/guardian should automatically be informed.

Examples:

**Payment recorded → Confirmation sent**

**Balance remains outstanding → Reminder sent**

**Payment deadline approaching → Reminder sent**

**Balance cleared → Payment-complete notification sent**

**Incorrect/reversed payment → Notification sent**

**Refund recorded → Refund notification sent**

This communication must happen automatically without requiring staff to manually send a message each time.

#### 8. Notification Safety and Accuracy

The system must ensure that financial and student information is only sent to the **authorized parent/guardian or staff member associated with that student/account**.

The system should:

* Keep notification records
* Prevent duplicate notifications where possible
* Track delivery status
* Log the date and time each notification was generated and sent
* Allow administrators to review communication history
* Never expose one student's information to another parent/guardian
* Allow authorized administrators to configure reminder frequency and notification channels

#### 9. Notification Dashboard

Create a dedicated dashboard showing:

* Total notifications sent
* Successfully delivered
* Failed notifications
* Pending notifications
* SMS usage
* Email usage
* Recent payment notifications
* Upcoming event reminders
* Outstanding fee reminders
* Scheduled notifications
* Communication history

The entire notification system must be fully connected to the **students, parents/guardians, staff, fees, payments, attendance, examinations, events, accounts, and school administration modules** so that important information is communicated automatically and accurately.
