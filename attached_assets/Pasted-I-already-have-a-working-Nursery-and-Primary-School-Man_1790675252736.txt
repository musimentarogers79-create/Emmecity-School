I already have a working **Nursery and Primary School Management System for a school in Uganda**, and pupils are already registered in the system.

**DO NOT rebuild the pupil registration system and DO NOT create duplicate pupil accounts.**

The main problem that needs to be solved is how the existing registered pupils are handled when:

1. They pay fees for Term 2.
2. They pay fees for Term 3.
3. They have unpaid fees from a previous term.
4. They leave/transfer to another school before a term ends.
5. They are promoted from one class to another at the end of the academic year.
6. Some pupils repeat the same class.
7. A pupil leaves and should no longer appear as an active pupil in the next term/year.
8. A pupil returns after previously leaving.

The system must be redesigned/updated so that **academic year, term, enrollment, fees, payments, promotion, repetition and transfers work together correctly while preserving all historical records.**

---

# 1. KEEP THE EXISTING PUPIL ACCOUNT

Every pupil already registered in the system must keep their existing:

* Pupil ID
* Registration number
* Name
* Date of birth
* Parent/guardian information
* Contact information
* Profile information
* Previous academic records
* Previous fee records
* Previous payment records

Never create a new pupil simply because:

* A new term starts.
* A new academic year starts.
* The pupil changes class.
* The pupil is promoted.
* The pupil repeats.
* The pupil transfers and later returns.

The pupil should have **one permanent profile** and multiple academic enrollments/history records.

---

# 2. THREE TERMS EVERY ACADEMIC YEAR

The system must properly support:

**Academic Year → Term 1 → Term 2 → Term 3**

Each term must have its own:

* Fee charges
* Payments
* Outstanding balances
* Payment history
* Class enrollment
* Attendance
* Academic records
* Reports

Do not mix Term 1, Term 2 and Term 3 financial records together.

For example:

### 2026

**Term 1**

* Fees charged
* Payments
* Balance

**Term 2**

* Fees charged
* Payments
* Balance

**Term 3**

* Fees charged
* Payments
* Balance

The system must allow a pupil to have a different payment status in each term.

---

# 3. TERM 2 FEES

When Term 2 begins, the system must automatically recognize that the pupil already exists.

The administrator should be able to open:

**2026 → Term 2 → Fees**

and see all pupils who are active/enrolled for Term 2.

For each pupil show:

* Term 2 fees required
* Previous outstanding balance
* New Term 2 charges
* Total amount due
* Amount paid for Term 2
* Total payments received
* Current outstanding balance
* Credit/overpayment

Example:

Pupil: John

Term 1:

* Fees: UGX 500,000
* Paid: UGX 400,000
* Balance: UGX 100,000

Term 2:

* Term 2 fees: UGX 500,000
* Previous balance: UGX 100,000
* Total amount due: UGX 600,000
* Paid Term 2: UGX 300,000
* Outstanding: UGX 300,000

The system must clearly show which amount belongs to which term.

---

# 4. TERM 3 FEES

Do exactly the same for Term 3.

When Term 3 begins, the system should automatically create/assign the appropriate Term 3 fee charges to pupils who are enrolled and active for Term 3.

For example:

### Term 3

Previous balance: UGX 200,000
Term 3 fees: UGX 500,000
Total due: UGX 700,000

If the parent pays UGX 250,000:

Paid: UGX 250,000
Outstanding: UGX 450,000

The payment must be recorded against the correct pupil and term.

Do not make the administrator manually create a completely new pupil record for Term 2 or Term 3.

---

# 5. PAYMENTS MUST BE TERM-SPECIFIC

Every fee payment must contain at least:

* Pupil ID
* Academic year
* Term
* Amount
* Payment date
* Payment method
* Receipt/reference number
* Staff member who recorded it
* Notes where necessary

The system should never simply add money to a pupil's general balance without identifying the relevant term.

The administrator must be able to open a pupil and see:

### Fee History

2026 Term 1
Charged: UGX 500,000
Paid: UGX 400,000
Balance: UGX 100,000

2026 Term 2
Charged: UGX 500,000
Paid: UGX 500,000
Balance: UGX 0

2026 Term 3
Charged: UGX 500,000
Paid: UGX 200,000
Balance: UGX 300,000

---

# 6. PREVIOUS BALANCES MUST NOT DISAPPEAR

If a pupil enters Term 2 with an unpaid Term 1 balance, the system must not erase it.

Instead show:

**Previous Term Balance**

and

**Current Term Charges**

separately.

This is extremely important because the school must be able to determine exactly which term generated the outstanding amount.

---

# 7. PUPIL LEAVES BEFORE THE TERM ENDS

The system must properly handle pupils who leave/transfer to another school before the end of Term 1, Term 2 or Term 3.

For example:

A pupil is enrolled in:

**2026 Term 2 — Primary 4**

Term 2 fees = UGX 500,000

The pupil has paid:

UGX 200,000

The pupil leaves the school during Term 2.

The system must NOT:

* Delete the pupil.
* Delete their payments.
* Mark the fees as paid.
* Automatically remove the outstanding balance.
* Create a duplicate pupil.
* Continue treating the pupil as an active pupil after their leaving date.

Instead, create an official **Transfer/Withdrawal process**.

---

# 8. TRANSFER/LEAVING PROCESS

When the administrator selects:

**Transfer/Withdraw Pupil**

the system should request:

* Leaving date
* Academic year
* Current term
* Current class
* Reason for leaving
* Destination school, if available
* Remarks
* Staff member processing the transfer

Then change the pupil's current enrollment status to:

**Transferred** or **Withdrawn**

The pupil should no longer appear in normal active-pupil lists.

However, the pupil must remain searchable in:

**Former Pupils / Transferred Pupils / Withdrawn Pupils**

---

# 9. FEES WHEN A PUPIL LEAVES

When a pupil leaves before the term ends, the system must **not automatically cancel the outstanding fees**.

Instead, calculate and display:

* Fees charged
* Amount paid
* Outstanding amount
* Credit balance, if any
* Adjustments
* Refunds, if any

The system should allow an authorized administrator to decide what happens according to the school's financial policy.

Possible actions:

* Leave balance outstanding
* Adjust/cancel approved amount
* Record refund
* Apply credit
* Record settlement
* Write off an approved amount

Every adjustment must have:

* Amount
* Reason
* Date
* Authorized user
* Notes
* Audit record

Never silently delete the financial record.

---

# 10. VERY IMPORTANT: DO NOT CHARGE A TRANSFERRED PUPIL IN FUTURE TERMS

If a pupil leaves during Term 2:

They may have:

**Term 1 — historical record**

**Term 2 — active until leaving date**

But they should NOT automatically receive:

**Term 3 charges**

after they have officially transferred/withdrawn.

Likewise, if they leave before the next academic year, they should not automatically appear as an active pupil in the next academic year.

---

# 11. PROMOTION AT THE END OF THE YEAR

At the end of Term 3, create an:

**END-OF-YEAR / PROMOTION PROCESS**

Do not manually change the pupil's class in a way that destroys their previous class history.

For example:

2026:
Pupil = Primary 3

At the end of 2026:

**Promote → Primary 4**

The system should create a new academic enrollment:

2027:
Pupil = Primary 4

But the pupil's permanent profile remains the same.

The history should show:

**2026 — Primary 3 — Completed/Promoted**

**2027 — Primary 4 — Active**

---

# 12. PROMOTION MUST NOT CHANGE OLD RECORDS

If a pupil was in Primary 3 during 2026, changing them to Primary 4 for 2027 must NOT change historical records.

Their 2026 records must still show:

* Primary 3
* 2026
* Term 1
* Term 2
* Term 3
* Results
* Attendance
* Fees
* Payments
* Outstanding balances
* Teacher comments

The new class applies only to the new academic enrollment.

---

# 13. PUPILS WHO REPEAT

Some pupils may not be promoted.

The administrator should be able to select:

**Repeat Class**

Example:

2026:
Primary 4

Decision:

**Repeat Primary 4**

Then the system creates:

2027:
Primary 4 — Repeating

Do not create another pupil.

Do not delete the previous Primary 4 records.

The pupil's history should clearly show:

2026 — P4 — Completed
2027 — P4 — Repeating

---

# 14. BULK PROMOTION

Because the school may have many pupils, provide a **Promotion Management** page.

The administrator should be able to see:

| Pupil | Current Class | Academic Year | Decision | Next Class |
| ----- | ------------- | ------------- | -------- | ---------- |
| John  | P3            | 2026          | Promote  | P4         |
| Mary  | P3            | 2026          | Repeat   | P3         |
| Peter | P3            | 2026          | Transfer | —          |

Allow the administrator to:

* Promote individual pupils
* Promote an entire class
* Mark pupils as repeating
* Mark pupils as transferred
* Correct individual decisions
* Review all decisions
* Confirm the promotion process

Do not finalize the process until the administrator confirms it.

---

# 15. PROMOTION SHOULD BE BASED ON SCHOOL DECISION

Do not automatically promote every pupil simply because Term 3 has ended.

The system should allow the school to enter/review:

* Final results
* Teacher recommendation
* Academic performance
* Promotion decision
* Administrator/headteacher approval

Then the authorized user confirms:

**Promoted**

or

**Repeating**

or

**Transferred**

or another approved status.

---

# 16. NEW ACADEMIC YEAR

When the administrator starts a new academic year, provide:

**Start New Academic Year**

The system should not copy everyone blindly.

Instead, it should use the previous year's final status.

For example:

### 2026 final status

John → Promoted
Mary → Repeating
Peter → Transferred
Sarah → Graduated

When creating 2027:

John → P4
Mary → P3
Peter → Not active
Sarah → Completed

Only eligible pupils should become active pupils for the new year.

---

# 17. FEES FOR PROMOTED PUPILS

When John moves from P3 to P4:

His 2027 P4 fee structure should be applied to his new 2027 enrollment.

Do not continue charging him using the old P3 fee structure.

Example:

2026 P3 fee = UGX 500,000

2027 P4 fee = UGX 550,000

The system should automatically use:

**2027 P4 = UGX 550,000**

while preserving all 2026 P3 financial history.

---

# 18. OUTSTANDING FEES WHEN PROMOTING

If a pupil is promoted while owing money from the previous academic year, the outstanding amount must not disappear.

Example:

2026 Term 3:

Fees = UGX 500,000
Paid = UGX 300,000
Balance = UGX 200,000

Pupil is promoted to P4 in 2027.

The system should show:

**Previous Academic Year Outstanding: UGX 200,000**

Then:

**2027 P4 Fees: UGX 550,000**

**Total Amount Due: UGX 750,000**

But keep the two amounts separately identifiable.

The school should always know that UGX 200,000 came from 2026 and UGX 550,000 is the 2027 charge.

---

# 19. IF A PUPIL WITH OUTSTANDING FEES LEAVES

If a pupil transfers while owing fees, their financial record must remain.

Example:

2026 Term 2:

Charged = UGX 500,000
Paid = UGX 250,000
Outstanding = UGX 250,000

Pupil transfers.

The system should show:

**Status: Transferred**

**Outstanding School Balance: UGX 250,000**

Do not delete the balance.

Do not automatically mark it as zero.

Provide a separate financial action for authorized staff if the school later receives payment, approves a waiver, or records another settlement.

---

# 20. IF A FORMER PUPIL PAYS LATER

The system must allow the school to receive payment from a pupil who has already transferred or left.

Search for the former pupil and record the payment against the original outstanding fee.

Example:

Former pupil:

2026 Term 2 balance = UGX 250,000

Later payment:

UGX 100,000

The system updates:

Original balance = UGX 250,000
Later payment = UGX 100,000
Remaining balance = UGX 150,000

Do not create a new pupil.

# 23. IMPORTANT DATABASE LOGIC

Use separate records for:

### Pupil

Permanent identity.

### Enrollment

Academic year + term + class + stream + status.

### Fee Charge

What the pupil was charged.

### Payment

What the school actually received.

### Financial Adjustment

Approved waiver, refund, correction, credit or write-off.

### Promotion History

Previous class → new class.

### Transfer/Withdrawal

Leaving date and status.

This is important because changing the pupil's current class or status must never overwrite previous records.

---

# 24. PREVENT DUPLICATE CHARGES

The system must prevent the same pupil from receiving duplicate fee charges for the same:

**Academic Year + Term + Fee Category + Enrollment**

unless an authorized user deliberately adds an additional approved charge.

This must be enforced at the database level as well as in the interface.

---

# 25. PREVENT DUPLICATE PROMOTION

A pupil must not accidentally be promoted twice.

If the pupil has already been processed for:

**2026 → 2027**

the system should recognize this and prevent another promotion record from being created accidentally.

Provide a controlled correction option for authorized administrators.

When closing a term, the system should automatically prepare:

* Fees charged
* Payments received

* Credits
* Pupils who left
* Active pupils

* Withdrawn pupils

Do not delete or lock away historical records.

They must remain available for reports and financial statements.

**2026 Term 1 → 2026 Term 2 → 2026 Term 3 → 2027 → 2027 Term 1 → Term 2 → Term 3 → 2028...**

without manually recreating pupils.

The system must automatically preserve the complete history while allowing:

**Term fees → payments → balances → transfers → promotion → repetition → new academic year → new term fees**

to work as one connected process.

Most importantly:

**DO NOT DELETE EXISTING PUPILS.**

**DO NOT CREATE DUPLICATE PUPILS.**

**DO NOT DELETE OLD FEE RECORDS.**

**DO NOT MIX DIFFERENT TERMS' PAYMENTS.**

**DO NOT LOSE OUTSTANDING BALANCES.**

**DO NOT CHARGE PUPILS AFTER THEY HAVE OFFICIALLY LEFT.**

**DO NOT CHANGE OLD CLASS HISTORY WHEN PROMOTING A PUPIL.**

Make the existing system capable of handling the school's real-life operations continuously and accurately.
